Station 06

Health screen

These are the standard pre-exercise screening questions used across the Australian exercise science profession. Answer honestly — if you're unsure about any of them, answer yes.

Nobody gets excluded for a yes. A yes means we take one extra step — usually a quick word with your GP — before we start loading you up. It's how the program stays safe.

Pre-exercise screening
01Has your doctor ever told you that you have a heart condition, or have you ever suffered a stroke?
02Do you ever experience unexplained pain or discomfort in your chest at rest or during physical activity?
03Do you ever feel faint, dizzy, or lose balance during physical activity?
04Have you had an asthma attack requiring immediate medical attention at any time in the last 12 months?
05If you have diabetes (type 1 or type 2), have you had trouble controlling your blood glucose in the last 3 months?
06Do you have any diagnosed muscle, bone or joint problem that you've been told could be made worse by physical activity?
07Do you have any other medical condition that may make it dangerous for you to participate in physical activity?

Medical clearance needed before we start

You've answered yes to at least one screening question. Before your first session you'll need written clearance from your GP, or we agree a documented modification in writing. Keep going with the form — I'll walk you through it when we speak.

Additional screening
08Are you currently pregnant, or have you given birth in the last 12 months?
09Has a medical practitioner ever told you to avoid or modify physical activity?
10Do you have high blood pressure or high cholesterol, diagnosed or being treated?
11Is there a family history of heart disease or sudden cardiac death before age 55 (male) or 65 (female)?
12Have you had a relative-energy or disordered-eating issue, or do you currently restrict food intake?
